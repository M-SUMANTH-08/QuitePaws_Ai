# QuietPaws — 90-Second Walkthrough

## 0–8 s — Problem
> “You're on an important client call. Your dog gets up and starts walking toward you. You can't leave the meeting. You can't unmute.”

> “So I made the phone handle the interruption.”

## 8–13 s — Reveal
Show QuietPaws on the POCO X6 Pro.
Overlay: `Development/demo device: POCO X6 Pro · Target: iQOO 15`.

## 13–25 s — SEE + HEAR
Show camera + workspace + audio telemetry.
> “QuietPaws combines visual and audio evidence around the workspace instead of reacting to a single signal.”

## 25–35 s — THINK
Show state + approach.
> “The phone decides whether this is becoming a meeting interruption.”

## 35–47 s — ACT
Phone plays the owner cue.
> “The first intervention is the owner's voice.”

## 47–57 s — VERIFY
Show `OBSERVING 2.6s` then `RESPONSE VERIFIED`.
> “It doesn't assume the intervention worked. It verifies the response.”

## 57–68 s — ADAPT
Scenario B: first cue fails → alternate cue → verified response.
> “If the first cue doesn't work, it tries one alternative. Then it stops.”

## 68–76 s — OFFLINE
Turn on airplane mode.
Show local camera/mic/model/intervention/haptic status.
> “The interaction loop does not require cloud inference.”

## 76–84 s — iQOO target
> “This prototype is running on my POCO X6 Pro. The product target is the iQOO 15. I will only report iQOO-specific performance after measuring it on the target hardware.”

## 84–90 s — Close
> “I never left the meeting. My phone saw my dog, understood what was happening, acted, verified it, and told me silently.”

> **“Your dog heard you leave. Your phone stayed to protect your meeting.”**

## Rules
- Never present a controlled scenario as a live animal measurement.
- Never claim POCO FPS/latency as iQOO FPS/latency.
- Never call browser FFT audio YAMNet.
- Do not use “emotion detection.”
- Use measured metrics only.

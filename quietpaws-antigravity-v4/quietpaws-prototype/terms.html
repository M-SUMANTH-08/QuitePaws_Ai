Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Prototype only

QuietPaws is a hackathon prototype and is not a veterinary, medical, safety, security, or behavioral diagnostic system.

Observable behavior only

The system estimates observable behavioral signals. It does not claim to read an animal's emotions or diagnose distress, aggression, pain, or illness.

Human responsibility

Users remain responsible for their animal, meeting, environment, and decisions. Do not use the prototype as a substitute for appropriate supervision.

Intervention

Auditory cues are demonstration mechanisms. QuietPaws does not guarantee that a dog will respond to a cue.

Target hardware

The product is designed for iQOO 15. The current browser prototype is developed/tested on a POCO X6 Pro. Target-device performance claims are only made after measurement.
